# LLMIC Design System

## Direction

- Product: immersive public science museum.
- Visual language: 1971 engineering drawing × dark cleanroom.
- Experience: one guided action per scene, with local free exploration.
- Primary audience: curious non-specialists aged 12+; technical depth remains optional.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#090b0d`; raised surface: `#111519`; line: `#293036`.
- Text: `#f4f0e8`; secondary: `#a8b0b5`.
- Silicon: `#a8c7c4`; diffusion: `#84b88b`; polysilicon: `#e36d55`; metal: `#77a7d9`.
- Accent: `#e6ff65`; evidence: `#cabdff`.
- Spacing follows 4/8px increments. Touch targets are at least 44px.

## Type

- Chinese UI: system sans with Noto Sans SC fallback.
- Display: high-contrast serif with Noto Serif SC fallback.
- Data and scale: system monospace.
- Body text is at least 16px with 1.6 line height.

## Interaction

- Motion expresses assembly, scale, or cause-and-effect; no decorative camera flights.
- Micro-interactions use 180–280ms timing and transform/opacity only.
- Keyboard and visible button alternatives accompany every gesture.
- Reduced motion replaces travel with crossfades and keeps all information available.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, semantic buttons and live chapter announcements.
- The 3D canvas is decorative to assistive technology; equivalent text is always present.
- Material categories use labels and shape/texture, never color alone.
